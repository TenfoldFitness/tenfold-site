10WHERE THIS COMES FROM

Two years of running it before selling it

From May 2023 to May 2025 I ran this method as a pilot in Danbury, CT. Groups of three to six women trained together five sessions a week on twelve-week cycles, four groups running back to back, with support groups held outside the training sessions. Participants could re-enrol for another cycle, and many did. What you get in the portal is that program, written down.

01

What was measured

Weight and measurements every four weeks, attendance at every session, confidence self-ratings, and a post-program questionnaire.

02

Who took part

Women in groups of three to six, across four groups. I have not published a total head count, so I do not quote one.

03

What it is not

A pilot, not a clinical trial. Self-reported outcomes from small groups, described here exactly as they were collected.
