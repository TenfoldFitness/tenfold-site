Where this comes from

Across two years of pilot cohorts, participants reported better emotional control, better sleep, and a better ability to work through a negative thought.

Self-reported by women who took part in a pilot in Danbury, CT, from May 2023 to May 2025. A pilot, not a clinical trial.

What was run

Groups of three to six women trained together five sessions a week on twelve-week cycles, with support groups outside the sessions.

Who took part

Four groups. I have not published a total head count, so I do not quote one.

What it is not

Not a clinical trial. Self-reported outcomes from small groups, described exactly as they were collected.

How it was measured See the details

Weight and measurements every four weeks, attendance at every session, confidence self-ratings, and a post-program questionnaire at the end of each cycle. Participants could re-enroll for another cycle, and many did.

The thinking behind the method Read the framework

Three needs, autonomy, competence and relatedness, from self-determination theory, plus six guiding ideas. Not a ranking and not a score. The full framework.

Questions

What people ask before starting.

Is this therapy? Open

No, and I want to be precise about that. Tenfold is skills coaching and physical training, not therapy and not medical care. The skills come from motivational interviewing and DBT distress tolerance. I hold an M.S.W. and an M.B.A. from Boston College with ten years of practice as a social worker behind them. I am not currently licensed and I do not practice clinically here. If something you bring me belongs with a licensed provider, I will tell you so and help you find one.

What do I get for $20 a month? Open

Portal access, the full Maximum Strength and Size program day by day with every lift written at three tiers, the fridge-to-recipe and restaurant macro tools, and a session log that keeps your own check-ins. No coaching calls at this level. Cancel anytime, no contract, no cancellation fee.

What if I am not "in shape" yet? Open

That is exactly who this is for. Every plan starts from where you actually are, not where you think you should be.

Do I need equipment? Open

Not necessarily. Programs can be built around resistance bands, dumbbells, or just your bodyweight.

Can I change or cancel later? Open

Monthly plans can be adjusted or cancelled any time. The 12-week packages are one payment, refundable only before your first session. Read the details.
