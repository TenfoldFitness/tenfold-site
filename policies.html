Cancelling and refunds

Monthly plans (Foundation and Guided)

There is no contract and no cancellation fee. You can cancel any time from your client portal. Cancelling stops future monthly charges.

12-week packages (Virtual Intensive and In-Person)

  • One payment. You pay once for the whole package.
  • It runs for 12 weeks, which is 84 days from the day you buy it. Your package access ends after that.
  • Refunds: you can have a full refund if you ask before your first coaching session. Once your first session has taken place, packages are not refundable.

If you are not sure a package is right for you, the free 15-minute call and the free session on this site exist so you can find out before you pay. If cost is the barrier, ask about the sliding scale.

Your information

What I collect

  • Free session: your email address, nothing else.
  • Booking a free call: your name, email, phone number, top priority, and the date you pick.
  • Joining: your name, email, phone number, top priority, and a password. Your password is stored in scrambled form by the login service, so I cannot read it.
  • In your portal: your starting assessment, your check-ins and workout log, your appointments, your saved restaurants, and a record of your plan.
  • Payment: you type your card into a secure form run by Square. I never see or store your full card number.

Why I collect it

To build and run your program, book and remind you about sessions, take payment, and reply to you. Your priority and assessment answers are used to shape your plan.

Who can see it

I can. So can the services that run the site: hosting, the database, payments and email. I do not sell your information and I do not share it for advertising.

Deleting it

You can ask me to delete your account and your information at any time. I will do it, except for payment records I am required to keep.

What you share here is used for coaching. It is not part of a medical record.

What Tenfold is and is not

Tenfold is skills coaching and physical training. It is not therapy and it is not medical care. I am not currently licensed and I do not practise clinically here. If what you bring me belongs with a licensed provider, I will say so and help you find one.

Check with your own provider before starting an exercise program if you have a medical condition or an injury.

The results on this site come from a small pilot and were self-reported. They describe what participants said, not what will happen for you.

Contact

The way to reach me is the free consultation form. Tell me your question when you book and I will reply by phone or email.
