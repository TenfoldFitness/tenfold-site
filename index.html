Questions people ask before starting

Do I need any equipment? ▼

Not necessarily. Programs can be built around resistance bands, dumbbells, or just your bodyweight, whatever you have access to.

What if I'm not "in shape" yet? ▼

That's exactly who this is for. Every plan starts from where you actually are, not where you think you should be.

Can I change or cancel my plan later? ▼

Yes. Monthly plans can be adjusted as your needs change, so you're never locked into something that no longer fits.

Is the mental health side real, qualified support? ▼

Yes. It's delivered by someone formally trained with real clinical experience, not a fitness coach offering casual advice on the side.
